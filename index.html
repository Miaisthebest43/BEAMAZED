01 · The Product

What are we selling?

An original invention designed to improve quality of life — and make homework suck less.

🍪 The Snack Sync 3000

A smart desk device that sits next to your homework and dispenses a small snack every time you complete a problem or focus for 10 minutes.

Parents load it with healthy snacks and set the goals. It turns boring homework into a rewarding game — you solve, you snack, you stay motivated.

  • Turns homework into a reward loop
  • Grades go up · stress goes down
  • Parents finally stop nagging
  • Runs on pure motivation (and crackers)

🎯 Why it works

The core idea: the brain loves snacks more than it loves fractions. So we bribed the brain.

Scene 1

Life Before

≈35s

LIGHTS UP on a messy desk. Sad violin music plays. STRUGGLING SAM sits slumped in the chair, staring at a mountain of homework. A phone buzzes. Sam picks it up, scrolls, then throws it down.

SAM(groaning, head on desk)"Ugh… homework. Why does it exist? I'd rather… I'd rather watch paint dry. And then watch it dry AGAIN."

Sam picks up a pencil, tries to write, erases violently, crumples the paper, and throws it across the room. Pause. Sam stares blankly at the wall.

SAM(deadpan)"I've been staring at this same problem for forty-five minutes. I don't even know what a fraction IS anymore."

FRUSTRATED FRAN enters, holding a cup of coffee, looking exhausted.

FRAN"Sam. Sweetie. It's 8 PM. Did you finish your homework?"
SAM(guilty, hiding papers)"…Define 'finish.'"
FRAN(rubs temples, sighs deeply)"Every. Single. Night. With this kid."

Sam flops face-down on the desk. TOTAL DEFEAT. Freeze frame. Sad violin SWELLS.

NARRATOR(V.O., dramatic)"Does this look familiar? The homework struggle… is REAL. But what if homework… could be delicious?"

LIGHTS CUT. Music stops abruptly. Beat of silence.

Scene 2

The Product Is Introduced

≈40s

LIGHTS FLASH ON. Upbeat, loud infomercial music BLASTS. NARRATOR bursts onto stage, sunglasses on, pointing dramatically.

NARRATOR"INTRODUCING… the revolutionary, the incredible, the one-and-only… SNACK SYNC 3000!!"

Narrator reveals the product — the decorated box with a snack bowl.

NARRATOR"Just load it with your favorite snacks… set your homework goal… and GET TO WORK! Every time you solve a problem — DING! — a snack drops right out!"

Sam sits up, suddenly VERY interested.

SAM"Wait. Hold on. You're telling me… I get a SNACK… for doing HOMEWORK?"
NARRATOR"That's right, Sam! Watch this!"

Sam picks up a pencil, solves an imaginary math problem with exaggerated effort, then SLAMS the pencil down.

SAM"DONE!"

🔔 DING! A snack drops into the bowl. Sam's eyes go WIDE.

SAM(whispering, amazed)"…It's beautiful."

Sam solves another problem. DING! Another snack. Sam solves faster and faster — DING! DING! DING! — snacks flying everywhere. Sam cackles with joy.

SAM(manic)"I LOVE HOMEWORK! I'M A GENIUS! I'M A SNACK GENIUS!!"
Scene 3

Life After

≈35s

Music shifts to a happy, bouncy tune. Sam is now calm, smiling, snack in one hand, pencil in the other, homework neatly stacked. Fran enters, stops dead in her tracks.

FRAN(shocked, hand on chest)"Sam… you're… you're DONE with your homework?! It's only 5 PM!"
SAM(cool, confident)"Yep. Finished an hour ago. I got a snack for every problem. I'm basically a genius now."

Sam dramatically holds up the test with A+ written in big red marker.

FRAN(gasps, then FAINTS backward into a chair — recovers instantly)"My baby's a straight-A student!"

HAPPY HANNAH steps forward for her testimonial.

HANNAH"I used to HATE homework. Now? I actually look forward to it! The Snack Sync 3000 changed my life!"

DISTRACTED DAVE steps forward, holding his own A+ test.

DAVE"I went from a C-minus student… to an A student. And I've never been happier… OR more full."(pats stomach)
Scene 4

The Offer

≈25s

NARRATOR steps to center stage, holds up the product, points at the audience.

NARRATOR"Now, how much would you pay for better grades, happier parents, and snacks ON DEMAND? $100? $200?"

Beat.

NARRATOR"NO! The Snack Sync 3000 is yours for just 3 easy payments of $19.99!!"

Narrator leans in, whispers intensely.

NARRATOR"But WAIT — there's MORE! Order within the next 10 MINUTES, and we'll throw in a FREE Snack Refill Kit — a $20 value — PLUS a bonus mini Snack Sync for your backpack! That's a $40 value — absolutely FREE!"

Narrator holds up the "FREE GIFT" sign and the mini box.

NARRATOR"CALL NOW! Operators are standing by!"
Scene 5

The Stipulations

≈20s

NARRATOR reads FAST and FUNNY, like a legal disclaimer — then slows down for the Florida part.

NARRATOR"Snacks NOT included. Not available in schools that ban snacks. May cause excessive studying, improved grades, and uncontrollable happiness. Do not use if allergic to snacks. Side effects may include happy parents, sudden love for math, and an addiction to learning. Not responsible for snack overconsumption. Void where prohibited. Results may vary. The Snack Sync 3000 is not a substitute for actual brain power."

Beat. Narrator takes off sunglasses, looks directly at the audience.

NARRATOR"NOT AVAILABLE IN FLORIDA."

Dead serious pause.

NARRATOR(whispering)"Florida… we love you. But between the alligators, the sinkholes, the humidity, and whatever Florida Man is doing this week… we simply cannot insure this product there."
NARRATOR(leaning in)"If you live in Florida and you still want one — good luck, godspeed, and please do not feed the device to a gator. Also not available in any state shaped like a hand. You know who you are. Stop asking."

Beat. Narrator puts sunglasses back on, smiles brightly.

≈25s

Entire cast lines up center stage. Music kicks in. Everyone sings and dances — simple choreography: sway, point, snack-drop hands.

ALL"SNACK SYNC 3000!!"

LIGHTS OUT. End.

🎒 Props

  • Bag of snack crackers / cereal
  • Phone
  • "FREE GIFT" sign + fake mini box
  • Optional: confetti, narrator sunglasses

🔊 Sound Cues

  1. Sad violin — Scene 1
  2. Record scratch silence — end of Scene 1
  3. Upbeat infomercial music — Scene 2
  4. DING! × 3–5 — Scene 2
  5. Happy bouncy tune — Scene 3
  6. Dramatic offer music — Scene 4
  7. Jingle backing — Scene 6 (or a cappella)

🎯 Pro Tips

  • The DING! is the star. Real bell, phone sound, or someone yelling it off-stage.
  • Sam's physical comedy carries Scene 1. Go BIG.
  • Narrator = loud and shameless in Scenes 2 & 4 — then dead serious for Florida in Scene 5. The tonal shift is the joke.
  • Take sunglasses OFF for the Florida line, put them back on after. It lands.
  • Everyone does the "snack drop" hand motion on every DING in the finale.
